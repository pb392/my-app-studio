From curious to playing

You don't need to know everything to take a turn.

Your first game is a chance to learn. Give yourself room to ask questions, read the cards and try again.

Pick a starting point ↑
  1. 01

    Follow your curiosity

    Choose a game whose art, creatures or world interests you. Use its official tutorial to see a turn in action.

  2. 02

    Learn with a deck that's ready

    Try a tutorial deck, borrow a deck, or ask for a beginner demo. Save building your own deck for after you know the basics.

  3. 03

    Make the first game a practice game

    Play slowly with someone happy to explain. Read unfamiliar cards together and focus on one new thing each turn.

A little guidance goes a long way

A friendly face for your first adventure.

First Draw brings a welcoming guide to a hobby full of unfamiliar names and possibilities. Start small, follow the creatures that catch your eye, and let your confidence grow one game at a time.

Deck building and deeper strategy can come later. Today, your next step is simply to begin.
