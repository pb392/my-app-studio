# Lesson 01 · Give the app a front door

**Goal:** make a branded homepage with AI, then explain how a visible choice
serves a visitor. Use the [README](../../README.md) for startup. Read only the
current step with your coach; you do not need to prepare every answer first.

## Try

See the neutral FastAPI homepage on the printed local URL. Tell the coach one
thing you notice. Agree APP-001: a homepage, simple SVG logo and shared styles.
Accounts come next. Open one issue and feature branch in your own repository.

**Checkpoint:** the page runs locally and the issue describes this small slice.

## Decide

Reuse your IS117 brand, or choose one visitor/use situation, a name, promise and
one primary brand archetype. The coach can give a small example; you can adopt
an idea and explain why it fits. You do not need a full branding report.

Before the plan, predict one effect of a visible choice on your visitor. A guess
is useful. Save the agreement in SPEC.md; unresolved choices stay pending.

**Checkpoint:** you can name who the page is for and one choice that helps them.

## Build

Review a short plan, then let AI apply the identity to the homepage, simple SVG
mark and shared styles. Keep controls clear and useful. Do not invent testimonials
or customer claims. Leave the terminal running so saved edits appear in the browser.

## Check

AI runs the cumulative APP-001 checks and reviews desktop/narrow layouts and
keyboard use. Unfinished acceptance checks must be fixed in the implementation,
not removed. The coach shows one actual check and asks what bug it would catch.

Then try the actual page yourself. Describe one visible detail and whether it
helps the intended visitor. The AI's browser observation is separate from yours.

**Checkpoint:** actual test evidence and your observation are recorded separately.

## Keep

Tell the coach whether to keep or revise, and why. Review the short LEARNING.md
summary before publication. Keep real evidence links in SPEC and your issue.
Review the checked PR, decide whether to merge, and verify its CI/merge/issue state.
Return to local main and pull the merge before checking progress.

**Done:** checked implementation, actual learner contributions and verified
GitHub completion. Next, the coach offers APP-002 rather than building it silently.
